• Android Auto, built in: wired or wireless, in the Auto screen's panel or full screen, with touch, the assistant and its turns on Revv's map.
• Revv wakes your Android phone over Bluetooth as you get in, and reconnects a session that stalls.
• Exit in Android Auto brings you back to Revv, like Revv's icon in CarPlay.
