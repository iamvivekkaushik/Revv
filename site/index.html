Car launcher · Android head units

The home screen your head unit should have shipped with.

Revv replaces your Android head unit's launcher with a dashboard built for driving: live gauges from the engine, maps and directions, calls from your phone, the rear camera and your apps, each one tap away and sized for a glance.

Download the APK Source on GitHub

Free · Android 9 or newer · No account, no ads, no tracking

Illustration of the home screen with demo figures.

What it does

Everything for the drive, on one screen.

Big targets, high contrast and nothing that needs a second look. Tap a card to go deeper; the dock is always there to come back.

LIVE OBD-II GAUGES

Plug a low-cost ELM327 adapter into the car's diagnostic port and the cluster comes alive.

  • Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi adapters
  • Speed, rpm, load, coolant, intake air and battery
  • A gear indicator from your gearbox ratios, or learnt as you drive
  • Fault codes in plain English, pending codes, and clearing them
  • Low battery and check-engine warnings

MAPS AND DIRECTIONS

OpenStreetMap maps drawn in the cluster's own style, with the next turn shown right on the home screen.

  • Search for places, addresses and areas
  • Turn-by-turn routes, with tolls avoided if you like
  • North-up or heading-up, and your recent places

YOUR PHONE, OVER BLUETOOTH

Revv reads the paired phone's calls and contacts the way a car kit does, and has the phone place calls for you.

  • Recent calls, favourites and contacts with A–Z quick scroll
  • A T9 dialer that finds people as you type
  • A call bar with an end-call button while you talk

REAR CAMERA

The head unit's own camera, live, with parking guide lines over the picture.

  • Picks a USB or external camera first
  • Rotate the picture and switch cameras
  • Guide lines on or off

APPS AND PROJECTION

Every installed app is a tap away, and phone projection starts from its own screen.

  • Start Android Auto, or the projection app you choose
  • Now playing from your music app on the home screen
  • Turn the fuel card into a shortcut to any app

MADE FOR YOUR CAR

Set it up once for your car and your screen, then forget about it.

  • Choose which cards and panels the home screen shows
  • Display size from 80% to 130%, date and time formats
  • Dims at sunset or with the light sensor
  • Tap, dialer and keyboard sounds, each on or off

Optional companion app

CarPlay, inside Revv.

Install the free RevvCarPlay companion and your iPhone's CarPlay runs in a panel on Revv's Auto screen, touch included, and keeps playing while you use the rest of Revv. Wired or wireless, set up from Revv's own settings.

  • Android 11 or newer on the head unit
  • Your own Apple accessory identity: the companion ships none
  • Use it with the Revv APK: the companion only takes its settings from a Revv signed with the same key, which the Google Play build isn't

Before you install

What you need.

Android

9 or newer

Built for landscape head units, from 16:9 screens to wide 1920×720 ones.

OBD-II

ELM327

Optional. Without an adapter, a demo drive shows what the gauges do. OBD-II support is new: tell us how it goes with your adapter and car.

Get one on Amazon: budget pick · reliable pick

Phone

Bluetooth

Pair your phone with the head unit and allow it to share contacts and call history.

Internet

For maps

Map tiles, routes and place search come from online OpenStreetMap services.

Give your head unit a proper home screen.

Install Revv from Google Play, or sideload the APK, then pick Revv when Android asks which app should be the home app. For CarPlay, add the companion app next to the APK.
