Car launcher · Android head units

The home screen your head unit should have shipped with.

Revv replaces your Android head unit's launcher with a dashboard built for driving: live gauges from the engine, maps and directions, CarPlay or Android Auto from your phone, its calls, the rear camera and your apps, each one tap away and sized for a glance.

What it does

Everything for the drive, on one screen.

Big targets, high contrast and nothing that needs a second look. Tap a card to go deeper; the dock is always there to come back.

CARPLAY AND ANDROID AUTO, BUILT IN

Plug your phone in over USB, or pair it and let it join the head unit's Wi-Fi, and its CarPlay or Android Auto runs in a panel on Revv's Auto screen, touch included, or over the whole screen. It keeps playing while you use the rest of Revv, and the Auto screen's PHONE card switches between the two. Nothing else to install on the head unit.

  • Wired over USB, or wireless over Wi-Fi Direct or the car's hotspot
  • Android Auto wakes your phone over Bluetooth as you get in, and reconnects by itself
  • The phone's next turn shows on Revv's map; its music shows on the home screen
  • CarPlay needs your own Apple accessory identity, which Revv does not ship
  • Free software in Revv's source: DiPlay's CarPlay stack (GPL-3.0) and DiAuto's Android Auto stack (AGPL-3.0)

LIVE OBD-II GAUGES

Plug a low-cost ELM327 adapter into the car's diagnostic port and the cluster comes alive.

  • Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi adapters
  • Speed, rpm, load, coolant, intake air and battery
  • A gear indicator from your gearbox ratios, or learnt as you drive
  • Fault codes in plain English, pending codes, and clearing them
  • Low battery and check-engine warnings

MAPS AND DIRECTIONS

OpenStreetMap maps drawn in the cluster's own style, with the next turn shown right on the home screen.

  • Search for places, addresses and areas
  • Turn-by-turn routes, with tolls avoided if you like
  • North-up or heading-up, and your recent places

YOUR PHONE, OVER BLUETOOTH

Revv reads the paired phone's calls and contacts the way a car kit does, and has the phone place calls for you.

  • Recent calls, favourites and contacts with A–Z quick scroll
  • A T9 dialer that finds people as you type
  • A call bar with an end-call button while you talk

REAR CAMERA

The head unit's own camera, live, with parking guide lines over the picture.

  • Picks a USB or external camera first
  • Rotate the picture and switch cameras
  • Guide lines on or off

APPS AND NOW PLAYING

Every installed app is a tap away, and what's playing is on the home screen.

  • Now playing from your music app, with seeking by drag or swipe
  • Turn the fuel card into a shortcut to any app
  • A projection app of your own, if the head unit came with one

MADE FOR YOUR CAR

Set it up once for your car and your screen, then forget about it.

  • Choose which cards and panels the home screen shows
  • Display size from 80% to 160%, with compact layouts for big text
  • Date and time formats
  • Dims at sunset or with the light sensor
  • Tap, dialer and keyboard sounds, each on or off

ENGINE SOUND Experimental

A synthesised engine through the head unit's speakers that follows your car's revs, load and gear changes from the OBD-II adapter, or the demo drive. It's made the way the open-source engine-sim makes its sound, and stays off until you turn it on.

  • Eleven engines from an inline 3 to a V12, with a boxer, an odd-fire V6, and cross- and flat-plane V8s
  • Four exhausts, from stock to a straight pipe
  • Pops on lift-off if you like, and a rev preview to try it parked
  • Plays under your music, with its own volume, and goes quiet for calls

Before you install

What you need.

Android

9 or newer

Built for landscape head units, from 16:9 screens to wide 1920×720 ones.

OBD-II

ELM327

Optional. Without an adapter, a demo drive shows what the gauges do. OBD-II support is new: tell us how it goes with your adapter and car.

Get one on Amazon: budget pick · reliable pick

Phone

Bluetooth

Pair your phone with the head unit and allow it to share contacts and call history. For CarPlay or Android Auto, plug it in over USB or let it join the head unit's Wi-Fi.

Internet

For maps

Map tiles, routes and place search come from online OpenStreetMap services.

Give your head unit a proper home screen.

Install Revv from Google Play, or sideload the APK, then pick Revv when Android asks which app should be the home app.
